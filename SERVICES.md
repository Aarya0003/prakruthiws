## 1. Driving School & Licence Services

### 1.1 Driving Classes & DL Support

**Description:** Professional driving lessons for two-wheelers and four-wheelers with experienced instructors. We provide comprehensive training to help you pass your driving test confidently.

**Required Documents:**
- Age proof (18+ for LMV, 16+ for two-wheeler)
- Address proof
- Medical certificate (if applicable)

**Process:**
1. Contact us to schedule your first lesson
2. Complete documentation
3. Begin practical training sessions
4. Prepare for RTO driving test
5. Get assistance with DL application

---

### 1.2 Learner's Licence (LL)

**Description:** Get your learner's licence processed quickly. We handle all documentation and RTO coordination for both two-wheeler and four-wheeler learner permits.

**Required Documents:**
- Age proof (Birth certificate, School certificate, or Passport)
- Address proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or Utility bill)
- Passport-size photos (recent)
- Form 2 (Application for Learner's Licence)

**Process:**
1. Submit required documents to us
2. We verify and prepare application
3. Book RTO slot for test
4. Appear for written test at RTO
5. Receive Learner's Licence

**Important:**
- LL is valid for 6 months
- Must hold LL for minimum 30 days before applying for permanent DL
- Age requirement: 18 years for LMV, 16 years for two-wheeler

---

### 1.3 New Driving Licence (DL)

**Description:** Complete assistance for obtaining your permanent driving licence after your learner period. We guide you through the entire process including the driving test.

**Required Documents:**
- Valid Learner's Licence
- Address proof
- Passport-size photos
- Form 4 (Application for Driving Licence)

**Process:**
1. Complete minimum 30 days with LL
2. Submit documents to us
3. Schedule driving test at RTO
4. Pass practical driving test
5. Collect permanent Driving Licence

**Validity:**
- Valid until holder turns 50 years (then renewable for 5-year periods)
- Valid for transport vehicles until holder turns 45 years

---

### 1.4 Driving Licence Renewal

**Description:** Renew your expired or expiring driving licence hassle-free. We handle all paperwork and RTO formalities.

**Required Documents:**
- Existing DL (expired or expiring)
- Address proof
- Passport-size photos
- Medical certificate (for commercial vehicles or if age 50+)

**Process:**
1. Submit existing DL and documents
2. Complete medical test if required
3. We prepare renewal application
4. Submit at RTO
5. Receive renewed DL

**Important:**
- Can apply up to 1 year before expiry or within 5 years after expiry
- After 5 years of expiry, may need to reappear for test

---

### 1.5 International Driving Permit

**Description:** Get your International Driving Permit for driving abroad. Valid in over 150 countries following the Geneva Convention on Road Traffic.

**Required Documents:**
- Valid Indian DL (at least 1 year old)
- Passport copy (valid)
- Visa copy (if available)
- Passport-size photos
- Form 4A (Application for IDP)

**Process:**
1. Submit required documents
2. We prepare IDP application
3. Submit at RTO
4. Collect IDP (usually same day)

**Validity:**
- Valid for 1 year from date of issue
- Must be accompanied by valid Indian DL when driving abroad

## 2. Vehicle Documentation Services

### 2.1 New Vehicle Registration

**Description:** Complete registration services for new two-wheelers and four-wheelers. We handle all RTO formalities for permanent registration from temporary registration.

**Required Documents:**
- Invoice from dealer
- Insurance certificate (comprehensive or third-party)
- Chassis and engine number rubbing
- Form 20 (Application by dealer)
- Form 21 (Sale certificate)
- Address proof
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)

**Process:**
1. Receive temporary registration from dealer (valid 30 days)
2. Submit documents to us within 30 days
3. We prepare permanent registration application
4. Pay road tax and registration fees
5. RTO inspection (if required)
6. Receive permanent RC

**Fees Include:**
- Registration fee
- Road tax (one-time or 15-year)
- Number plate charges
- Smart card fee

---

### 2.2 Vehicle Ownership Transfer

**Description:** Transfer vehicle ownership when buying or selling a used vehicle. We ensure smooth RC transfer with proper documentation.

**Required Documents:**
- Original RC book/card
- Insurance certificate (valid)
- Sale agreement (notarized)
- Form 29 (Notice of transfer by transferor)
- Form 30 (Notice of transfer by transferee)
- Address proof of new owner
- NOC from financer (if loan exists)

**Process:**
1. Seller and buyer provide documents
2. We prepare transfer forms
3. Both parties visit RTO (or authorized center)
4. Pay transfer fees
5. Updated RC issued in new owner's name

**Important:**
- Transfer must be done within 14 days of sale (to avoid penalty)
- Pending challans must be cleared before transfer
- Insurance must be valid

---

### 2.3 Duplicate RC

**Description:** Lost your RC book? Get a duplicate Registration Certificate issued quickly with our assistance.

**Required Documents:**
- FIR copy (if stolen) or Affidavit (if lost)
- Insurance certificate (current)
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Form 26 (Application for duplicate RC)
- Vehicle photos

**Process:**
1. File FIR if stolen, or prepare affidavit if lost
2. Submit documents to us
3. We prepare duplicate RC application
4. Pay duplicate RC fees
5. Collect duplicate RC from RTO

**Fees:**
- Duplicate RC fee: As per RTO rates
- Smart card fee: As applicable

---

### 2.4 Hypothecation Removal

**Description:** Remove loan hypothecation annotation from your RC after completing vehicle loan payment. Clear your RC to show full ownership.

**Required Documents:**
- Original RC book/card
- NOC from bank/financer (original)
- Loan closure certificate
- Form 35 (Application for change in RC)

**Process:**
1. Obtain NOC from your bank/financer
2. Submit documents to us
3. We prepare hypothecation removal application
4. Submit at RTO
5. Receive updated RC without hypothecation

**Important:**
- NOC from bank is mandatory
- Process usually takes 7-15 working days
- No test or inspection required

## 3. Insurance & Compliance Services

### 3.1 Motor Insurance Support

**Description:** Assistance with motor insurance purchase, renewal, and claim processing for all vehicle types. We guide you through coverage options and documentation.

**Required Documents:**
- RC book/card copy

---

### 3.2 Fitness Certificate (FC)

**Description:** FC renewal for commercial vehicles. We coordinate vehicle inspection and certificate issuance with RTO.

**Required Documents:**
- Original RC book/card
- Insurance certificate (valid)
- Pollution certificate (valid PUC)
- Tax receipt (road tax paid)
- Form 22 (Application for fitness certificate)

**Process:**
1. Ensure vehicle is in good condition
2. Submit documents to us
3. Schedule fitness test at RTO
4. Vehicle inspection by RTO officer
5. Receive FC if vehicle passes

**Validity:**
- 2 years for new commercial vehicles (less than 8 years old)
- 1 year for commercial vehicles over 8 years old
- FC must be renewed before expiry

**Important:**
- Vehicle must be physically present for inspection
- All lights, brakes, horn, mirrors must be functional
- Carry fire extinguisher and first-aid box

---

### 3.3 Pollution Under Control (PUC)

**Description:** Get your PUC certificate for emission compliance. Required for all vehicles every 6 months to ensure environmental standards are met.

**Required Documents:**
- RC copy
- Previous PUC certificate (if any)

**Process:**
1. Bring vehicle to authorized PUC center
2. Emission test conducted
3. Certificate issued immediately if passed

**Validity:**
- 6 months for petrol vehicles
- 3 months for diesel vehicles (older vehicles)
- Must be carried while driving

**Important:**
- PUC is mandatory - can be fined if not carried
- Get tested well before expiry
- Available at numerous centers across the city

## 4. Transfer & Commercial Services

### 4.1 NOC & Re-registration

**Description:** Interstate vehicle transfer assistance. We obtain NOC from original RTO and handle re-registration in Karnataka.

**Required Documents:**
- Original RC book/card
- Insurance certificate (valid)
- Address proof in Karnataka (residence proof)
- NOC from original RTO
- Form 27 (Application for assignment of new registration mark)
- Form 28 (Notice of transfer of ownership)

**Process:**
1. Apply for NOC at original RTO (we assist)
2. Submit NOC and documents to Karnataka RTO
3. Pay new registration fees and road tax
4. Vehicle inspection at Karnataka RTO
5. Receive new RC with KA registration number

**Important:**
- Original state road tax must be clear
- No pending challans in original state
- Process takes 15-30 days
- Vehicle must be present for inspection

---

### 4.2 Commercial Permits

**Description:** Obtain commercial permits for taxis, goods vehicles, auto-rickshaws, and transport vehicles. Required to legally operate commercial vehicles.

**Required Documents:**
- RC book/card copy
- Insurance certificate (commercial policy)
- Fitness certificate (valid FC)
- Route permit application (if applicable)
- Form 46 (Application for permit)
- Tax receipts

**Types of Permits:**
- Stage carriage permit (buses)
- Contract carriage permit (tourist vehicles, taxis)
- Goods carriage permit (trucks, tempo)
- Auto-rickshaw permit
- Private service vehicle permit

**Process:**
1. Determine type of permit needed
2. Submit documents to us
3. Apply at RTO with required documents
4. Inspection if required
5. Receive permit

**Validity:**
- Usually 5 years (renewable)
- Must be renewed before expiry

## 5. RTO Services

### 5.1 Address Change in DL/RC

**Description:** Update your address in driving licence or vehicle registration documents when you relocate within Karnataka or to/from another state.

**For DL Address Change:**
- Original DL
- New address proof
- Form 9 (for DL changes)
- Passport-size photos

**For RC Address Change:**
- Original RC
- New address proof
- Form 18 (for RC changes)
- NOC from financer (if loan exists)

**Process:**
1. Submit documents to us
2. We prepare change of address application
3. Submit at RTO
4. Receive updated DL/RC with new address

**Important:**
- Must update within 30 days of address change
- For out-of-state transfer, NOC process required
- Aadhaar address is preferred proof

---

### 5.2 Name Change in Documents

**Description:** Update name in DL or RC due to marriage, legal name change, or correction of spelling errors.

**Required Documents:**
- Original DL/RC
- Name change gazette notification OR marriage certificate
- Affidavit (notarized)
- Proof of name change (from official source)
- Form 9 (for DL) or Form 18 (for RC)
- Passport-size photos (for DL)

**Process:**
1. Obtain gazette notification for name change (if applicable)
2. Prepare affidavit
3. Submit documents to us
4. We prepare name change application
5. Submit at RTO
6. Receive updated documents

**Important:**
- Gazette notification process takes time - plan ahead
- For minor spelling corrections, affidavit may suffice
- All vehicle documents should have consistent name

---
